# Pattern: Introduction (warm or cold)

Use when: first contact with someone, or introducing two parties.

## Structure
1. Who I am and why I'm writing — one sentence, no biography
2. Why this is relevant to them specifically — what's in it for them
3. One low-friction ask — a short call, a question, a reaction

## Key rule
The relevance sentence is everything. Generic intros get ignored.
Reference something specific: their work, a mutual connection, a recent news item.
