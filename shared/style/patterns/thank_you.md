# Pattern: Thank you / post-meeting

Use when: after a meeting, call, demo, or someone did something useful.

## Structure
1. Thank them — one sentence, reference what specifically was valuable
2. One concrete recap point or confirmation (optional, only if useful)
3. Next step — what happens now and who owns it

## Key rule
Be specific. "Thanks for the call" is meaningless.
"The comparison between [product A] and [product B] specs was exactly what I needed" is real.
