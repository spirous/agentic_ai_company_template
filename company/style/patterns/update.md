# Pattern: Status update / proactive share

Use when: sharing news, a progress update, or something relevant they should know.

## Structure
1. The news — lead with the fact, not "I wanted to share"
2. Why it matters to them — one sentence connecting it to their situation
3. Optional ask or invitation — "let me know if useful", "happy to discuss"

## Key rule
Updates should feel like a gift, not a check-in.
If you don't know why they'd care, don't send it.
