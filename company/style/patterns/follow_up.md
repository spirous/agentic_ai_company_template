# Pattern: Follow-up (no response)

Use when: you sent something and got no reply, or a next step stalled.

## Structure
1. One sentence referencing what was sent / discussed — no apology, no "just checking in"
2. One sentence on why it matters now (timing, deadline, their interest)
3. One clear ask — reply, confirm, schedule

## Example shape
"[Reference to last touch] — [why now]. [Single ask]?"

## Key rule
Do not send the same email twice. If the first follow-up got no response,
change the angle: try a different ask, a shorter message, or a new hook.
