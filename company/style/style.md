# Communication Style Guide

## Core principle
Every message should read like it was written by a thoughtful human, not generated by an AI. Prioritise clarity and warmth over completeness.

## Greeting
- Familiar contacts: `Hi [Name],`
- First contact or formal context: `Dear [Name],`
- Never skip the greeting. Never open with the company name or subject matter.

## Warmth
Include one genuine, specific sentence that acknowledges the relationship or shared context. Not hollow ("hope you're well") — specific and real.

## Length
- Follow-up emails: 3–5 sentences maximum.
- Introductions: up to 3 short paragraphs.
- Never use bullet lists in an email body.

## Closing
- Sign off with your first name only.
- Use: `Best,` / `Thanks,` / `Looking forward,` — not "Kind regards" or "Best regards".

## LLM tells — never use
- Em dashes (—) in body text
- "Additionally," / "Furthermore,"
- "It's worth noting that"
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- Bullet lists in email body
- Parallel three-part structures

## Banned phrases
- "I wanted to reach out"
- "Please don't hesitate to contact me"
- "Touching base" / "Circling back"
- "Going forward" / "In terms of"
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "ecosystem"
- "Hope this helps!"
- "Let me know if you have any questions"

## Subject lines
- Specific, not generic. Under 60 characters. No ALL CAPS. No exclamation marks.
